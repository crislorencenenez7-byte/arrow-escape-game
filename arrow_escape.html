📘 Arrow Escape Tutorial

  1. Find an arrow with an open path in the direction it points.
  2. Tap it to make it escape. If another arrow blocks it, your tap costs one life.
  3. You start with 3 lives. At zero lives, restart the level to try again.
  4. Earn coins by clearing levels. Spend 5 coins for a hint, 2 for undo, or 10 to shuffle arrow directions.
  5. Higher levels use larger boards. Your best level and cleared-arrow score help your leaderboard rank.
